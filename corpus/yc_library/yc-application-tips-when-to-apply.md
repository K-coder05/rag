When should you apply to YC? YC’s Stephanie Simon shares her insights.

Transcript

You should apply to YC when you're working on a startup and you think that your company would benefit from YC. There are many ways that YC can accelerate a startup's growth, and it's up to you as a founder to decide if and when YC is right for you. There's no such thing as too early or too late. Our application is always open and we read applications year round, so apply whenever the time is right for your company.
