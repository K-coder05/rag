YC Application Tips: You don't need to know someone
by Stephanie Simon
29K views

Do you need to know someone at YC to get in? Absolutely not, as YC’s Stephanie Simon explains.

Transcript

You don't need to know anyone at YC to get in. There's some bad advice on the internet that says you need a YC alumni recommendation. That is not the case. If a YC alumni knows you well and wants to chime in on your behalf, we'll gladly read it. But hundreds of startups getting every batch without a recommendation. And it's actually a negative signal when founders solicit a bunch of recommendations from alumni who barely know them. It can come across as spammy, so I just wouldn't do it.
